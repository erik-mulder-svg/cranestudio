Yiga Luganda Privacy Policy

Crane Studio, effective 10 October 2026

Yiga Luganda is a free app for learning Luganda. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. This page explains the little information the app handles and where it goes.

Information stored on your device

Your learning progress (completed lessons, XP, streaks, review history, Listen progress) and any word corrections you make are stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. We never receive this information. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone or any other permission-protected data.

Internet connections

The app connects to two services to load content:

  • Supabase hosts the Luganda audio recordings played in the Listen tab.
  • Google Fonts provides the typeface the app uses.

When your phone downloads these files, those services receive the technical information any website receives, such as your IP address and device type, and may keep it briefly in server logs under their own privacy policies (Supabase, Google). We do not receive or use this information.

Audio

Luganda audio in the app was generated with Sunbird AI's text-to-speech service while building the app. Using the app does not send anything to Sunbird AI.

Children

The app is meant for adult learners and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
