Yiga Luganda

Yiga means “learn”. Short lessons, real Luganda audio and graded stories take you from your first Oli otya? to everyday conversations in Kampala.

Coming soon to Google PlayAndroid, free

Inside the app

What you can do

Learn step by step

  • Units from greetings and numbers to past and future tenses
  • Word, matching and sentence-building exercises
  • A short grammar tip for every unit

Listen to real Luganda

  • Every sentence and story line voiced in Luganda
  • Slow mode for tricky sentences
  • Stories in ten levels, read along line by line

Keep it

  • A review tab that brings older words back more often
  • A list of every Luganda word in the app
  • Streaks and XP for daily practice

Privacy

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
